Help & Support

Everything you need to get started with CatOS.

Welcome to CatOS

CatOS helps you organise tasks, track progress, and stay motivated with your own virtual cat. Here's how to get started.

Go to Today →

Getting Started

01

Create a Task

Click Add task in the sidebar. Enter a title and optionally add a description, priority, start date, deadline, or labels.

02

Manage Your Tasks

Edit tasks, mark them as completed, or delete tasks you no longer need. Break larger tasks into smaller subtasks.

03

Organise with Labels

Create reusable labels to group related tasks. You can assign multiple labels to the same task.

04

Find Your Tasks

Use Search to find tasks by keyword, label, priority, or completion status. You can also view all your tasks in one place.

05

Track Your Progress

Visit Data to see your task completion rate and daily or weekly statistics. Check Today to follow your daily progress.

06

Meet Your Cat

Every account has its own virtual cat. Give your cat a name and complete tasks to increase its Mood by 5, up to 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Where can I find upcoming tasks?

Open Upcoming from the sidebar to view tasks scheduled for future dates.

What happens when a task is overdue?

Incomplete tasks that have passed their deadline are highlighted as overdue. You can change their dates or reschedule multiple overdue tasks.

Can I use multiple labels on one task?

Yes. You can select multiple existing labels or create new ones when adding or editing tasks.

Does completing a subtask complete its parent?

No. Parent tasks and subtasks have independent completion states.

How do I change my profile picture?

Open Settings and upload a PNG, JPG, JPEG, or WebP image.

Can other users see my tasks?

No. Your tasks and labels are associated with your account and are not shared with other CatOS users.
